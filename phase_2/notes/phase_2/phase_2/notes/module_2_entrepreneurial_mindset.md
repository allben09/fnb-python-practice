## 🎯 Key Concepts

### 1. The Entrepreneurial Mindset
- **Opportunity recognition** – spotting problems worth solving
- **Resourcefulness** – doing more with less
- **Resilience** – learning from failure
- **Calculated risk** – not gambling, but betting smartly

### 2. Business Foundations
| Concept | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Revenue | Money coming in |
| Cost | Money going out |
| Profit | Revenue − Cost |
| Break-even | Point where revenue = cost |
| Margin | Profit as % of revenue |

### 3. Business Models
- B2B (Business to Business)
- B2C (Business to Consumer)
- B2G (Business to Government)
- Non-profit / Social Enterprise

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

1. **Every problem is a business opportunity.**
2. **Numbers don't lie** – always know your break-even.
3. **Social enterprises** can be both profitable AND impactful.
4. **Start small, validate, then scale.**
