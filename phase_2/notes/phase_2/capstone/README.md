## 💡 My Capstone Idea (Draft)

**Project:** *Rural Connect – Community Services Directory*

**Problem:** Rural communities in Limpopo struggle to find nearby NPOs, NGOs, and government services.

**Solution:** A mobile app (built with Kivy) that:
- Lists local community organisations
- Filters by category and distance
- Allows users to contact organisations directly
- Works offline for low-connectivity areas

**Why this idea:**
- Connects directly to **Communi-Connect Umbrella** (my mother's organisation)
- Uses **Kivy** (Module 8)
- Uses **APIs** (Module 10)
- Solves a real South African problem
