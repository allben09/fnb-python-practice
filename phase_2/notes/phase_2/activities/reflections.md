## 📅 Module 1 – Digital Literacy

**Prompt:** *How do digital tools improve your productivity?*

**My Response:**

> Digital tools remove friction. Before, I would spend hours formatting documents and tracking tasks on paper. Now I use Trello-style boards (GitHub Projects), Google Docs for collaboration, and Canva for visuals. These tools save me at least 5 hours a week — time I reinvest into learning and building projects.

## 📅 Module 2 – Entrepreneurial Mindset

**Prompt:** *Identify a problem in your community and propose a business solution.*

**My Response:**

> **Problem:** Small NPOs in rural Limpopo struggle to access funding because they lack digital presence and compliance documentation.
>
> **Solution:** A subscription platform (or free service funded by corporates) that helps NPOs register, build a digital profile, and connect to donors. This is the model behind **Communi-Connect Umbrella**.
