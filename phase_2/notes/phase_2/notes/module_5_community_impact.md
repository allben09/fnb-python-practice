## 🎯 Key Concepts

### 1. Community Impact
The measurable, positive change an organisation creates within a community — social, economic, or environmental.

### 2. Inclusive Growth
Economic growth that is distributed fairly and creates opportunities for all — especially marginalised groups.

### 3. Measuring Impact

| Framework | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Theory of Change** | Maps inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact |
| **SROI** | Social Return on Investment — value created per rand spent |
| **Logical Framework** | Structured planning and evaluation matrix |

### 4. South African Context
- High youth unemployment
- Rural/urban digital divide
- GBV crisis
- Food insecurity in townships and rural areas

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

1. **Impact must be measured** – not just claimed.
2. **Include the excluded** – design for rural, disabled, and youth.
3. **Partnerships multiply impact** – no org works alone.
4. **Story + Data = Funding** – donors want both numbers and narratives.
