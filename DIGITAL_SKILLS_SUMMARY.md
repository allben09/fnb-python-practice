# 📈 Digital Skills & Business Transformation Summary

## Key Learnings from the FNB App Academy

### 1. The Role of Digital Skills in Finance
The programme emphasized how digital literacy is critical in modern banking. From mobile banking apps to AI-driven fraud detection, understanding technology is no longer optional—it's essential.

### 2. Python as a Business Tool
While Python is a programming language, the course showed how it serves as a strategic business tool for:
- Automating repetitive tasks.
- Analyzing financial data.
- Building prototypes for new digital products.

### 3. Problem-Solving Mindset
Financial institutions need problem solvers. The FNB programme focused on using technology to solve real customer pain points, such as:
- Improving financial inclusion in rural areas.
- Enhancing cybersecurity for customer data.
- Streamlining payment processes.

### 4. Future of Work
South Africa's job market is shifting. This programme prepared me to adapt to:
- AI and automation in the workplace.
- Remote and hybrid work environments.
- The need for continuous learning and upskilling.
